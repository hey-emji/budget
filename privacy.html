Privacy notice

Last updated November 11, 2026

bujjet is built so your financial information stays with you.

What we collect

Nothing. bujjet has no servers that store your data and no analytics or ad trackers. Everything you enter (income, expenses, balances, debts and settings) is saved in your browser's storage on your own device. You can use bujjet without any account.

Optional Google sign-in and Drive sync

If you choose to connect Google, bujjet asks Google for permission to see your name and email address (to show which account is connected) and to use a private app folder in your Google Drive. bujjet can only see the files it creates in that folder, not the rest of your Drive.

bujjet then saves a copy of your bujjet data to that folder, straight from your device to Google. It never passes through our servers, and we can't see it. The copy is protected by your Google account. Google's own privacy policy applies to data stored in your Drive.

The sign-in token that lets bujjet sync is kept on your device for up to an hour. You can disconnect any time in Settings → Google account, and remove bujjet's access completely in your Google Account settings under "Third-party apps and services". Disconnecting doesn't delete the copy in your Drive; you can delete it from Google Drive's settings under "Manage apps".

Backups

When you export a backup, bujjet creates a file on your device. If you set a password, the file is encrypted on your device with AES-256 before it's saved. We never see your backups or your password, so we can't recover a lost password.

Fonts and hosting

The app's files are served by our hosting provider, and its fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. These services may log standard technical information such as your IP address when your device downloads the app's files. They never receive your budget data.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Backup & data → Erase all data on this phone, or clear this site's data in your browser settings. Because we don't hold your data, there's nothing to delete on our side. If you connected Google, also remove the copy in your Drive as described above.

Contact

Questions about privacy: contact the bujjet developer through the channel where you got the app.
