Privacy notice

Last updated November 9, 2026

bujjet is built so your financial information stays with you.

What we collect

Nothing. bujjet has no accounts, no servers that store your data, and no analytics or ad trackers. Everything you enter (income, expenses, balances, debts and settings) is saved only in your browser's storage on your own device.

Backups

When you export a backup, bujjet creates a file on your device. If you set a password, the file is encrypted on your device with AES-256 before it's saved. We never see your backups or your password, so we can't recover a lost password.

Fonts and hosting

The app's files are served by our hosting provider, and its fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. These services may log standard technical information such as your IP address when your device downloads the app's files. They never receive your budget data.

Deleting your data

Use Budget → Erase all data on this phone, or clear this site's data in your browser settings. Because we don't hold your data, there's nothing to delete on our side.

Future features

If we add optional sign-in or sync later, we'll update this notice before it launches and ask for your consent. Sync is planned to save an encrypted copy to your own Google Drive, not to our servers.

Contact

Questions about privacy: contact the bujjet developer through the channel where you got the app.
